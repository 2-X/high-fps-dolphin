Heat-haze shimmer pace fix v1 (TShimmer BTK x0.25, self-gated)
GMSE01 (US). Companion to the fpspatch bundles; safe to leave enabled at stock
(self-disables when the framerate global is 0.5).

Symptom: the heat mirage (Delfino/Gelato/Noki ground shimmer) pulsates ~4x too
fast under any fpspatch bundle (120/180/240/360 alike — constant, not G-scaled).

Cause (family-B raw-rate class, same as the ANMRATE_SITES fixes in fpspatch.py):
TShimmer::perform (USA 0x8019F83C, matched from PAL 0x801980C4 size 0x288;
decomp sms-decomp/src/Map/Shimmer.cpp) advances its indirect-warp BTK on every
CUE_MOVE via a private J3DFrameCtrl at this+0x58 whose rate is pinned to 1.0 by
J3DFrameCtrl::init in TShimmer::load — it never passes through
SMSGetAnmFrameRate(), so the substep retune's MOVE cadence (120 Hz at every G,
vs stock 30 Hz) runs it 4x fast. Not reachable via ANMRATE_SITES (no rate store
exists to hook — init's 1.0 is the only writer), hence this dedicated hook.

MOVE block in perform:
  8019F88C  lwz  r4, 0x58(r29)      ; unk58 = J3DFrameCtrl
  8019F894  lfs  f0, 0x10(r4)       ; ctrl->mFrame
  8019F898  stfs f0, 0x4(r3)        ; btk->setFrame(frame)
  8019F89C  lwz  r3, 0x58(r29)      ; <-- C2 hook (re-exec orig, then rate store)
  8019F8A0  bl   0x802E1730         ; J3DFrameCtrl::update (mFrame += mRate @+0xC)

Hook body: after the original lwz (r3 = frame ctrl), load the framerate global
0x804167B8 (-0x3E8(r2), r2=0x80416B80) and the native 0.5f (-0x7FD8(r2)); if
equal (stock, codes off) skip; else store 0.25f into ctrl->mRate (+0xC).
Idempotent per-tick store, so it survives scene reloads (init re-pins 1.0, we
re-apply on the first MOVE tick before update runs). 120 Hz x 0.25 = stock
30 anim-units/s, fractionally interpolated by the BTK (smoother than stock).
Clobbers: f0/f13/r12/cr0 — all dead at the hook site (f0 last read at 8019F898,
cr0 redefined by the rlwinm. at 8019F8A4, r12/f13 volatile across the bl).

Not the FOV mod: the $FOV unified hook already substitutes the same fovy into
SMS_GetLightPerspectiveForEffectMtx (USA 0x8022BA74, caller-whitelisted at
C_MTXPerspective entry), so world-shimmer alignment tracks the render FOV.
Known residual: portal-preview passes render ~50 while the hook forces the
override — preview-only misalignment, documented in sunshine-fov-mod.md.
