Tank Controls v4 (heading integrator + neutral re-arm)
GMSE01 (US). Supersedes v3 (remove v3 when enabling v4).

Fixes over v3 (both user-reported):
1. Left/right dead in air / FLUDD hover / slides. Those states never
   rotate mFaceAngle toward mIntendedYaw the way ground running does;
   they use angleDiff = mIntendedYaw - mFaceAngle.y directly:
     - air (doJumping/slipFalling/etc): vel += k*cos(angleDiff),
       face += k*sin(angleDiff)*slideControl
     - hover: front/rear cone = thrust only; SIDE band (0x1555..0x6AAA)
       = face IConverge at mHoverParams.mRotSp + nozzle tilt
     - slides: steering force from sin(intendedYaw - unk9E)
   v3 derived intendedYaw from face each frame, so angleDiff <= 0xC0
   (~1deg) -> sin ~ 0 -> no steering authority in those states.
   v4 integrates the heading on mIntendedYaw ITSELF (persists at
   this+0x90): out = prev + clamp(theta>>6,+/-0xC0) - leak, where
   leak = (prev - face)>>6 slowly re-syncs the heading to the real
   facing (heals wall bonks/warps in ~0.2s) while allowing ~67deg of
   lead in air (sin ~ 0.92 -> full air/hover/slide authority).
   Ground feel unchanged: face tracks heading within ~1deg there.
2. Release-back then press-back went straight instead of re-flipping.
   The latch cleared only on steering frames; neutral stick takes the
   game's OWN else-branch (intendedYaw = face @80251de8) which our
   main hook never sees. Second micro-C2 there zeroes the latch, so
   letting go of the stick instantly re-arms the turnaround.

Hooks:
  C2 @ 80251DDC (36 words): main heading logic (regs r0/r3/r4 free,
     r30 = dizzy jitter, r31 = TMario*; latch timer at this+0x92 =
     vestigial unk92, see v3 notes).
  C2 @ 80251DE8 (4 words): re-exec `lha r0,0x96(r31)`, zero latch.
     (r4 is reloaded at 80251df0, safe to clobber.)

TUNING KNOBS (per-frame, tuned for G=6 / 360fps):
  steer shift 6 (7C843670) / cap 0xC0     -> turn rate
  leak shift 6 (7C003670)                 -> air lead cap ~67deg & resync speed
  window 0x168 (38600168)                 -> held-back re-flip period (1s)
  cone 0x6000                             -> turnaround threshold
